What the official record says

The policy is real. The local process is still uneven.

China's K Visa took effect on 1 October 2025. National sources confirm the audience and broad benefits, but many filing posts still do not publish a K-specific checklist.

Know what still needs an answer.

A credible guide should show uncertainty, not hide it. These are the questions to resolve before spending money or booking travel.

What counts as a recognized university or research institution?

No public universal list is linked in the national announcement. Prepare rankings, accreditation, and institution evidence, then confirm locally.

What age limit will my filing post apply?

The official national explanation refers to an age requirement but does not state one public universal number on the cited page.

Which documents need apostille, authentication or translation?

Document treatment depends on where the document was issued and where you file. Do not rely on a checklist written for another country.

Can I take a salaried job immediately after entry?

Treat employment as a separate compliance question. Ask the employer and local authorities whether a work permit or status change is required.

How long will the visa be valid?

The policy promises more convenience in entries, validity and stay, but the actual visa issued depends on the authorities and the individual case.
